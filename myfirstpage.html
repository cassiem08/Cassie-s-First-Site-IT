About Me

My name is Cassie, and I have always been passionate about computers—particularly the software that brings them to life. In fifth grade, I found joy in creating digital invitations for my parties, designing cards for family members, and even making banners to decorate my room. I especially loved it when teachers assigned us topics to research and present using PowerPoint; the process of combining creativity with technology fascinated me and sparked my interest in exploring the endless possibilities of software.

I also developed a strong passion for typing. Once my teacher discovered how much I enjoyed working on the computer, she arranged for me to receive private typing lessons with one of the school’s tech-savvy instructors, Mrs. Poore. She would pull me from class for short sessions, no longer than an hour, to teach me proper finger placement and techniques for typing both quickly and accurately. These lessons not only improved my technical skills but also deepened my enthusiasm for working with computers.

I've recognized my growth with my tech skills, and decided to take them a step further by learning the inner workings of computers as well. I am looking forward to completing the courses and eager to expand my knowledge in the field of IT!
